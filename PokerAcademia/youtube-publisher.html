Poker Academia Shorts Publisher

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Poker Academia Shorts Publisher uses YouTube API Services. It is an internal tool: its only user is the owner of the Poker Academia YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and cannot access any other channel.

What it does

Poker Academia publishes short educational poker quizzes (vertical videos of about 16 seconds) on its own YouTube channel, youtube.com/@Poker-Academia. The content is educational only: no real-money gambling, no betting links, no promises of winnings.

The Shorts Publisher automates the last step of that workflow, so that each approved video goes online at a regular time without a manual upload:

  1. Production. Each week, the quiz videos are rendered from our own poker training material.
  2. Human review. The channel owner watches every video on an internal review page and approves or rejects it. Nothing is uploaded without this approval.
  3. Upload. Once a day, the tool uploads the approved video of the day to the Poker Academia channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), with its title, description, tags, category and language.
  4. Log. The tool records the returned video ID and URL in the internal publication log, so the same video is never uploaded twice.

Access to YouTube data

  • Authorization: OAuth 2.0, granted once by the channel owner.
  • Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload only.
  • The tool does not read viewers' data, comments, analytics or any other channel.
  • Expected volume: 1 to 2 uploads per day, well within the default quota.
